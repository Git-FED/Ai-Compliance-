What this is—and isn't

A checklist, not an autopilot.

The pack is designed to help a human reviewer keep observations bounded, evidence traceable, and open questions visible.

Look for observable evidence

Record what was seen, where it came from, and when it was checked. Missing or uncertain evidence stays visible.

Keep a human in the loop

Legal interpretation, policy ambiguity, and live changes belong with qualified human review—not an automatic stamp.

Leave room for “not assessed”

Do not infer a pass from silence, a badge, or one screenshot. Check current official sources before relying on any future finding.

Draft reporting vocabulary

No points. No averaging.

The draft uses four descriptive outcomes. Each needs evidence and human judgment; none is a certification.

CLEAREvidence supports in-scope items; still not a certification or guarantee.
CONDITIONALA material question, evidence gap, or condition remains unresolved.
AT RISKEvidence suggests an issue needing prompt human attention.
BLOCKEDA verified hard stop requires current authoritative evidence and human confirmation.
Important: With no evidence reviewed, do not fill the gap with assumptions. Report “Not assessed” or stop for incomplete scope. Read the draft rating rules.

Keep the open decisions open.

Repository name, visibility, license, first store to review, paid-access choice, and approval of the draft remain unresolved. A license file is intentionally not included.
