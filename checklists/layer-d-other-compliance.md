# Layer D — Other compliance prompts (D1–D6)

> **Draft only; not legal advice.** These broad prompts are jurisdiction- and product-dependent. Confirm applicability and current authoritative sources with a qualified reviewer. No store has been reviewed.

| ID | Review prompt | Evidence / escalation cue |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Which consumer-protection and e-commerce disclosures may apply in each market served? | Identify seller identity, material terms, pricing, delivery, returns, complaints, and cancellation evidence; ask counsel to determine applicable legal duties. |
| D2 | What personal data may be collected through the storefront, analytics, ads, support, or checkout, and what public privacy information is provided? | Review only public notices and authorized, non-personal flow behavior. Do not collect personal records. Escalate lawful basis, notices, retention, rights, and cross-border questions. |
| D3 | Are product-safety, labeling, warranty, or regulatory claims relevant to the goods/services and target markets? | Identify category and claims; verify with current regulator sources and qualified experts. Do not infer safety or conformity from a badge. |
| D4 | Are accessibility barriers apparent in key public journeys, and what accessibility standard or law may apply? | Record observed barriers and test scope. Do not assert conformance from an automated or limited review; escalate to an accessibility specialist. |
| D5 | Are marketing, endorsements, testimonials, influencer content, and performance claims presented transparently and supportably? | Preserve exact public claim and context; check relevant current law/platform rules and substantiation with a qualified reviewer. |
| D6 | Is the product or business in a regulated or age-sensitive sector (for example, health, finance, alcohol, gambling, children, or professional services)? | Identify possible sector trigger and market. Stop making a generic conclusion until qualified, jurisdiction-specific review is complete. |
