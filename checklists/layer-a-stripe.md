# Layer A — Stripe review prompts (A1–A8)

> **Draft only; not Stripe policy guidance.** Verify every platform-related point against current official Stripe materials for the relevant country, business type, product, and account configuration. No store has been reviewed. These prompts do not establish that any item is a Stripe requirement.

For each item record: result (`CLEAR / CONDITIONAL / AT RISK / BLOCKED / Not assessed`), evidence reference(s), observation date, uncertainty, and human reviewer. No numeric score.

| ID | Review prompt | Evidence to look for / escalation cue |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Is the merchant/business identity presented consistently on public pages and at the payment point? | Compare displayed business name and public contact details across storefront, policies, and checkout. Do not infer account ownership or verify private Stripe records here. |
| A2 | Are product/service descriptions and prices clear before a customer is asked to pay? | Capture product page and checkout wording, currency, options, and any material add-ons. Flag discrepancies for human review. |
| A3 | Are shipping, delivery, fulfillment, or service-start expectations stated where relevant? | Look for timing, geography, fulfillment method, delays, and exceptions. Record absent or conflicting wording as evidence uncertainty, not automatic failure. |
| A4 | Are refund, return, cancellation, and dispute/contact instructions accessible and understandable? | Capture the applicable policy and how it is presented before purchase. Check current jurisdictional and platform sources separately. |
| A5 | If recurring or deferred charges are offered, are amount, frequency, trial/intro period, renewal, and cancellation terms visible before consent? | Inspect the public signup/checkout flow only as authorized. Escalate unclear consent, cancellation path, or surprise-charge indicators. |
| A6 | Are taxes, fees, shipping charges, and other material totals disclosed before the payment commitment? | Compare displayed price with checkout total in a permitted test or public view; never place an order or submit payment for this review. |
| A7 | Are products, claims, and business model described accurately and consistently across the storefront and payment-facing pages? | Compare ads/product pages/policies; flag unsupported claims, restricted-category questions, or mismatches for current official-source review. |
| A8 | Has the reviewer identified relevant current Stripe documentation and any account-specific questions that cannot be answered from public pages? | Cite the official source, region, and access date; label private account status as Not assessed unless separately authorized and securely reviewed. |
