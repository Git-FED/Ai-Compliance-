# Layer B — Meta review prompts (B1–B9)

> **Draft only; not Meta policy guidance.** Verify current official Meta policies and regional rules before any conclusion. These prompts do not establish eligibility, approval, or policy compliance. No store has been reviewed.

For each item record: result (`CLEAR / CONDITIONAL / AT RISK / BLOCKED / Not assessed`), evidence reference(s), observation date, uncertainty, and human reviewer. No numeric score.

| ID | Review prompt | Evidence to look for / escalation cue |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Does the public business identity match across the storefront, social profile, ad destination, and commerce surfaces in scope? | Capture public names, links, and contact/policy links; avoid collecting private account IDs. |
| B2 | Does the landing page deliver what the ad or social commerce listing appears to promise? | Compare dated public ad/listing copy with the destination page, price, product, and availability. |
| B3 | Are material terms (price, subscription, shipping, returns, cancellation, eligibility) accessible and consistent across the destination flow? | Record what a visitor can see before purchase; flag conflicting or hidden material terms. |
| B4 | Are product and promotional claims supported by available, appropriate evidence and presented without misleading implication? | Preserve exact claim and source; escalate health, finance, outcome, or other high-impact claims for qualified review. |
| B5 | Does the offer involve a product, service, audience, or claim that may fall under restricted or prohibited platform categories? | Identify the category question and consult current official policy for relevant placement/region; do not declare policy status from memory. |
| B6 | Are age, geography, or other eligibility limits stated and applied consistently where relevant? | Compare public terms, targeting description if authorized, and available visitor-facing controls; private targeting settings remain Not assessed unless authorized. |
| B7 | Are tracking technologies and personal-data uses described and controlled in a way that warrants jurisdiction-specific privacy review? | Record only publicly observable notices/consent behavior. Do not inspect or export personal-level event data. Escalate pixel/SDK and consent questions. |
| B8 | Are privacy and contact details accessible from the social/commerce journey and consistent with the destination? | Capture public policy links and relevant statements; verify legal sufficiency separately for each jurisdiction. |
| B9 | Has current official Meta guidance for the relevant product surface, ad placement, account type, and region been checked? | Cite exact official source, date, and scope. Account standing, review status, or enforcement history is Not assessed without separate authorization. |
