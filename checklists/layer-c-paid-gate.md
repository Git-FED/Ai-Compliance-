# Layer C — Paid-gate decisions (C1–C2)

> **Draft only.** Whether the tool itself is paid-gated is a blocking owner decision. This checklist does not authorize tool-access gating, charges, payment-detail collection, or a pricing model. Voluntary third-party supporter links on the landing page are separate: they do not unlock the tool and do not resolve this decision.
