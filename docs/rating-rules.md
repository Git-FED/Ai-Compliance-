# Rating rules (draft)

> **Draft only.** Ratings describe the reviewer's evidence-based view at a point in time. They are not scores, certifications, or guarantees.

## Allowed ratings

| Rating | Use when |
|---|---|
| **CLEAR** | In-scope evidence is sufficient and current for the limited question reviewed, and no unresolved material concern was found. Clearly state what was and was not checked. |
| **CONDITIONAL** | The conclusion depends on a stated condition, or a material fact/source is incomplete, ambiguous, stale, or conflicting. Name what would resolve it. |
| **AT RISK** | Available evidence points to a material exposure or likely issue requiring timely human attention. State the evidence and next review step without claiming a final legal determination. |
| **BLOCKED** | A verified hard stop is established for the in-scope question using current authoritative support and human confirmation. Explain exactly what is blocked and what would be needed to reopen review. |

Checklist entries may also be **Not assessed**. That is an item state, not an overall rating. If the review itself cannot begin because scope/authorization is incomplete, use the separate stop label **NOT RATED — SCOPE INCOMPLETE**.

## Worst-flag rule

The overall report takes the most severe supported finding among the in-scope checklist items: **BLOCKED > AT RISK > CONDITIONAL > CLEAR**. Do not average, count flags, or calculate a percentage. A single well-supported critical finding can govern; a single missing piece of evidence alone does not automatically create BLOCKED.

## Reporting discipline

- State the scope, jurisdiction assumptions, evidence cut-off date, exclusions, and material uncertainty.
- Cite evidence for every material finding; separate facts, interpretation, and recommendation.
- Never describe CLEAR as “compliant,” “approved,” “safe,” or “guaranteed.”
- Do not call a third-party platform's likely decision certain.
- Explain how a finding could change if the facts, law, source, or platform policy changes.
- A qualified human must review before external use or any live operational change.
