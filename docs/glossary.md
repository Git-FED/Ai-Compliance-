# Glossary (draft)

- **Access gate / paid gate:** A product decision that restricts some tool functionality to paid users. Whether this tool is paid-gated is unresolved.
- **Authoritative source:** The official law/regulator/platform source appropriate to the jurisdiction and question; it still must be checked for scope and freshness.
- **AT RISK:** Evidence indicates a material exposure or likely issue requiring prompt human attention; not a final legal finding.
- **BLOCKED:** A narrowly used rating for a human-confirmed, evidence-supported hard stop in scope.
- **CLEAR:** A limited evidence-based result for the questions checked; never a certification.
- **CONDITIONAL:** A material finding depends on an unresolved condition or evidence gap.
- **Evidence reference:** A non-sensitive pointer to a dated public source or approved secure record; it should not embed customer data or credentials.
- **Fictional self-test:** A workflow test using invented entities, facts, and sources, visibly labeled as fictional; it is not a real-store review.
- **Hard stop:** A verified in-scope condition that prevents proceeding under the adopted review criteria. Criteria require approval and must not be invented by the evaluator.
- **Jurisdiction:** A relevant country, state/province, territory, or other legally meaningful location; it must be established rather than guessed.
- **Material:** Important enough to affect the review's conclusion or a decision; reviewers must explain why.
- **Not assessed:** No conclusion is made because evidence was not reviewed or available. It does not mean pass or fail.
- **Worst-flag rule:** The most severe supported item finding governs the overall rating; no averaging or numeric score.
